Center for Academic Innovation

UX Researcher In progress

Ongoing UX research on learner expectations, early-stage concept evaluation, and insight synthesis. Work includes the Atlas Graduation Roadmap, a Michigan Online personalization feature, and Michigan Online wireframe testing.

The Influence of AI on Self-efficacy

Quantitative Researcher In progress

A survey-based study of how generative AI tools like ChatGPT affect young professionals' confidence in their own work abilities.

Designing for Youth Connectedness

Qualitative Researcher In progress

A participatory design study of how teens in Washtenaw County experience social connection, working toward design guidelines for well-being technologies.

Navigating Careers @ Michigan

UX Researcher Completed

Interviews and generative research into why University of Michigan students underuse career resources. Key factors: unclear communication, lack of personalization, and reliance on peers.

Buddy Connect

UX Researcher / UX Designer Completed

Research, prototyping, and usability testing for a platform that helps international and out-of-state students adjust to living on their own, delivered as a high-fidelity, developer-ready design.
