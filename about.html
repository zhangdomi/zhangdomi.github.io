About Me

I'm a mixed methods researcher with 3+ years of research experience, studying Psychology and Cognitive Science at the University of Michigan.

My interest in UX research grew out of a long-standing curiosity about how people behave and make decisions. I believe products are made by humans, for humans, so understanding people is where good design starts.
